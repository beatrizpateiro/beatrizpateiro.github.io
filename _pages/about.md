I received my PhD in Statistics and Operations Research at the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://www.usc.gal/en) in 2008, with a dissertation entitled "Set estimation under convexity type restrictions". After completing my PhD, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher (Ángeles Alvariño postdoctoral fellowship). Currently I am Associate Professor (Profesora Titular de Universidad) at the [Department of Statistics, Mathematical Analysis and Optimisation](https://eamo.usc.es) ([University of Santiago de Compostela](https://www.usc.gal/en)).

My research interests include set estimation and computational statistics.
