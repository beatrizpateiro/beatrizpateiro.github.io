I received my PhD in Statistics and Operations Research from the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://www.usc.gal/en) in 2008, with a dissertation entitled *Set estimation under convexity type restrictions*. I subsequently held an Ángeles Alvariño postdoctoral fellowship.

I am currently Associate Professor (*Profesora Titular de Universidad*) at the [Department of Statistics, Mathematical Analysis and Optimisation](https://investigacion.usc.gal/unidades/1921?lang=en), [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://www.usc.gal/en).

My research interests lie primarily in set estimation and computational statistics, with particular interest in methodological developments and their applications.
